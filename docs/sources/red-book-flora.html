What it is

The Red Book of Vascular Flora of the Republic of Kosovo was prepared by a team led by Fadil Millaku (editor-in-chief) with Ferat Rexhepi, Elez Krasniqi, Qazim Pajazitaj, Xhavit Mala and Naim Berisha, published by the Ministry of Environment and Spatial Planning in 2013 with support from German development cooperation (GIZ). Its English summary states that 237 taxa were assessed against the IUCN criteria: 1 Extinct, 1 Extinct in the Wild, 61 Critically Endangered, 86 Endangered, 19 Vulnerable, 34 Near Threatened and 35 Least Concern. The selection drew on the literature, on Administrative Instruction No. 18/2012 on protected species, and on the state of each species in the field.

The methods are worth noting for what they imply about data. For every species assessed, the team took herbarium samples, photographs and GPS coordinates, counted mature individuals where possible, and measured area of occupancy and extent of occurrence on the UTM grid. The locality data therefore exist, but they are not in the summary.

What it adds for Natura 2000 in Kosovo

The report already flags species on the EU annexes and on the global Red List. Neither captures what is threatened in Kosovo itself. A national list of 148 Critically Endangered or Endangered plants would let the report mark records of nationally threatened species, and let site selection favour candidate sites that hold them. The field coordinates behind the assessments would, if shared, be among the most precise plant data for the country. The list serves the national tier.

Caveats and sensitivity

  • The summary alone gives counts, not species.
  • National categories apply regional IUCN guidelines and cannot be compared one-to-one with global ones.
  • Several of the Critically Endangered taxa are small populations of endemics; their coordinates must never be published precisely.

Integration into the pipeline

Medium effort once a table exists: a proposed R/build_red_list.R, modelled on R/build_directive_list.R, would resolve the names and write data/kosovo_red_list_flora.csv; pipeline.R would then add a national category column to every plant record. pipeline.R is not changed here.
