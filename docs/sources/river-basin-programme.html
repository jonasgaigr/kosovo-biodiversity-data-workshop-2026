WarningTo be completed

The document could not be opened, so nothing about its content is verified. The desk review describes it as the ministry’s work programme for river basin management, 2025–2027. A search engine’s summary of the dead link describes a work programme and timetable for preparing river basin management plans for the “White Drin, Ibër, Morava, Binça and Lepen” rivers for 2028–2033; that summary is secondary and unverified.

River basin management plans matter for Natura 2000 because the Water Framework Directive requires a register of protected areas that includes water-dependent Natura 2000 sites, and because the plans’ programmes of measures decide what happens to the rivers that freshwater habitats and fish depend on.

A dead link is a small thing with a large consequence for evidence. When a ministry’s document moves without a redirect, every report that cited its address stops being checkable, and a search engine that still indexes the old address makes the document look available when it is not. That is why this page records the attempt and its date, and why the section as a whole keeps an evidence row for every value: a reader can see not only what was found but what could no longer be found.
