What it is

The Independent Commission for Mines and Minerals (ICMM, KPMM in Albanian) is Kosovo’s mining regulator. Its website offers application forms for exploration and mining licences, a “data portal”, a GIS link and a “data shop” selling printed maps of Kosovo at 1:50,000, 1:100,000 and 1:200,000. Its home page also summarises reserves of the main mineral resources: lignite, lead and zinc, nickel, bauxite and magnesite.

What it adds for Natura 2000 in Kosovo

Mining is a pressure that a site proposal must face early, because a licence granted inside a candidate site constrains its designation and management. Knowing where exploration and mining licences lie, and how they overlap the existing protected areas and the habitat screen, is part of the pressure analysis for an SDF (section 4.3, threats and pressures) and for any appropriate assessment. Licence polygons would serve the site tier.

Caveats and sensitivity

  • A login is required; the terms of access are unknown.
  • Licence areas are not mine footprints; they mark where activity is permitted.

Integration into the pipeline

Low effort with data: a proposed read_mining_licences(path) would clip the polygons and report overlaps with protected_areas. pipeline.R is not changed.
