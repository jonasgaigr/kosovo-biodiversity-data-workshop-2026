What it is

Libri i Kuq i Faunës së Republikës së Kosovës is Kosovo’s red book of animals. It was published in Prishtinë in 2018 by the Ministry of Environment and Spatial Planning, with the Kosovo Environmental Protection Agency (KEPA) and the Kosovo Institute for Nature Protection, edited by Halil Ibrahimi with nine co-authors from Kosovo and the region. The desk review cited it as Ibrahimi et al. 2019, which matches the PDF’s date of July 2019 rather than the imprint year of 2018. It runs to 203 pages in Albanian, with chapters by group (molluscs, oligochaetes, insects, fish, reptiles, birds and others), national IUCN categories, and a species account for each listed animal. Those accounts name the localities where each species is known in Kosovo and carry distribution maps.

Kosovo coverage check

The book is national by definition. The check that matters is whether its locality data exist as data. They do not in public: localities appear as place names in the text and as printed maps, and no table of records or coordinates is released. The desk review’s note that “locality data are not published” holds, with that nuance.

What it adds for Natura 2000 in Kosovo

A national red list says which animals are at risk in Kosovo itself, which neither the EU annexes nor the global IUCN list (both already in the report) can. Species that are nationally threatened and also on Annex II are obvious priorities for site selection; species threatened nationally but absent from the annexes point to where national protection must do what Natura 2000 will not. It serves the national tier.

Caveats and sensitivity

  • All rights reserved; the book can be cited and read, not republished.
  • Maps and named localities of threatened species are sensitive. Any use in this site must stay at 10 km or coarser for persecuted or collected species and state what was generalised.
  • Categories follow IUCN regional guidelines as applied by the authors; they are not comparable one-to-one with global categories.

Integration into the pipeline

Medium effort. A proposed data/kosovo_red_list_fauna.csv, holding species, national category and GBIF key only, built by an R/build_red_list.R like the existing R/build_directive_list.R, would let pipeline.R add a nationalRedList column to every record. That needs either the Ministry’s agreement or a table it publishes. pipeline.R is not changed here.
