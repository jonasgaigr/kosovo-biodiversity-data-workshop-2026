What it is

EMMA2, the second Atlas of European Mammals, maps the distribution of Europe’s mammals on a common grid. Its website gives the book’s publication date as 6 October 2026, one week after this check, with CRC Press, edited by Mitchell-Jones, Amori, Kryštufek and colleagues. It covers the area of the first atlas plus Moldova, Belarus and Ukraine, with Russia excluded. As of June 2024 the project database held 142,965 records of 245 species.

The desk review reported that Kosovo is included for the first time. The pages opened in this check do not say so, and that remains to be confirmed from the published atlas.

Kosovo coverage check

The executable part of the check is whether EMMA2 records are already on GBIF, which would mean the report’s GBIF extract might contain them.

Show the GBIF dataset search 
if (!file.exists(cache)) {
  q <- c("EMMA2", "Atlas of European Mammals", "European Mammal Foundation")
  hits <- do.call(rbind, lapply(q, function(x) {
    r <- tryCatch(jsonlite::fromJSON(paste0(
      "https://api.gbif.org/v1/dataset/search?limit=20&q=", utils::URLencode(x))),
      error = function(e) NULL)
    if (is.null(r) || !r$count) return(data.frame(query = x, title = NA, publisher = NA))
    data.frame(query = x, title = r$results$title,
               publisher = r$results$publishingOrganizationTitle)
  }))
  write.csv(hits, cache, row.names = FALSE)
}
hits <- read.csv(cache)
hits <- hits[!is.na(hits$title), ]
mammal <- hits[grepl("mammal", hits$title, ignore.case = TRUE), ]
is_emma2 <- grepl("EMMA2|Atlas of European Mammals", mammal$title)
cat(sprintf(paste("The three searches returned %d dataset titles. %d of them",
                  "concern mammals, and %s.\n\n"),
            nrow(hits), nrow(mammal),
            if (any(is_emma2)) "one of those is the EMMA2 atlas" else "none of those is the EMMA2 atlas"))

The three searches returned 49 dataset titles. 17 of them concern mammals, and none of those is the EMMA2 atlas.

No EMMA2 dataset was found on GBIF on 29 September 2026. Besides the mammal datasets listed, the searches return unrelated datasets that merely share the name, such as French observers’ personal datasets and a Madrid herbarium called EMMA.

One of the mammal datasets deserves a second look for a different reason: “Small mammals of Yugoslavia 1992 atlas”, published to GBIF by the National Museum of Natural History in Sofia. An atlas of Yugoslavia from 1992 would have covered Kosovo, and its records may carry a Yugoslav or Serbian country code rather than XK. Whether they fall inside Kosovo’s outline, and whether the report’s download already holds them, is listed below as an open check.

What it adds for Natura 2000 in Kosovo

Mammals on Annexes II and IV, bats above all, are thinly recorded in Kosovo’s GBIF data. A systematic atlas grid gives a presence baseline against which gaps can be judged, and a cross-check for species reported from only a handful of records. At the atlas grid it serves the national screening tier.

Caveats and sensitivity

  • The atlas is not yet published, and its data route is unknown.
  • Kosovo’s inclusion is reported by the desk review, not confirmed here.
  • Bat roosts and large carnivores need generalisation if finer data are ever supplied.

Integration into the pipeline

If EMMA2 appears on GBIF, nothing new is needed beyond deduplicating against the existing download. If it arrives as a grid table, the proposed read_atlas_squares() on the EBBA2 page applies. pipeline.R is not changed.
