What it is

Each year the European Commission assesses Kosovo’s progress towards the EU across the chapters of the acquis. The 2025 report was published on 4 November 2025. Its Chapter 27, on environment and climate change, finds Kosovo “at an early stage of preparation” with limited progress.

On nature protection the report is short and specific. Kosovo made limited progress. The identification of potential Natura 2000 sites “is at a very early stage”. Protected areas lack appropriate management and suffer from pollution, illegal activities and weak enforcement. There was no progress in aligning legislation on nature protection. The adoption of a strategy for environmental protection and sustainable development for 2024–2033 remains pending. On forests it notes that 40 per cent of public forests are subject to uncontrolled or illegal harvesting.

Kosovo coverage check

The report is about Kosovo alone; there are no data to check. The quotations above were read from the PDF on 29 September 2026.

What can be checked is consistency with the other pages. The Commission’s finding that identifying potential Natura 2000 sites is “at a very early stage” matches the absence of any candidate site list in this section (see Mustafa 2009 and the data gaps). Its note that the environment strategy for 2024–2033 is still pending matches the lack of a successor to the biodiversity action plan.

What it adds for Natura 2000 in Kosovo

It is the external benchmark. Whatever this site and the Ministry produce towards Natura 2000 will be read against the Commission’s next assessment, and the phrases above are the ones it will want to see changed: the identification of potential sites, protected-area management and legal alignment. It serves the national tier as context. The finding on illegal harvesting bears on the forest-loss and NFI pages.
