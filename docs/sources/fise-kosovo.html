What it is

The Forest Information System for Europe (FISE), run by the European Environment Agency, keeps country profiles for EEA members and cooperating countries, and Kosovo has one. Its indicators are the share of land covered by forest (2020), forest per capita (2020), growing stock and its density (2020), and gross annual increment (2015). The profile names its sources as the FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment, the FOREST EUROPE pan-European dataset, Eurostat and the EEA. It is therefore a secondary compilation of national reporting, not a dataset of its own.

What it adds for Natura 2000 in Kosovo

Little directly for site identification: these are national totals. They are useful as the internationally reported denominator for forest area, a check on the forest share in the report’s CORINE screen, and as a pointer to which international datasets already hold Kosovo forest data. That serves the national tier.
