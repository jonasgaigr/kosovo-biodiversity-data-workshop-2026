What it is

Specimens collected in Kosovo over the last two centuries sit in museum drawers in the region and in Central Europe. The desk review named the natural history collections in Tirana, Belgrade, Skopje, Vienna and Budapest as likely holders of Kosovo material outside GBIF. The desk review did not verify that, and this page is a lead rather than a dataset.

What can be verified is the GBIF side: which institutions already publish Kosovo specimens, and whether the five named ones publish at all.

Of the 61 publishers, 6 are based in the five countries the desk review named: University of Graz (AT); Department of Biology and Ecology, Faculty of Sciences, University of Novi Sad, Serbia (RS); University of Vienna - Herbarium WU (AT); Biologiezentrum Linz Oberoesterreich (AT); Natural History Museum, Vienna (AT); Steiermärkisches Landesmuseum Joanneum - Herbarium GJO (AT). Whether the specific museums in Tirana, Belgrade, Skopje, Vienna, Budapest publish Kosovo specimens under another name, or not at all, is the open question below.

The table answers a narrower question than the desk review asked, and the difference is worth keeping in view. It shows which institutions already publish preserved specimens that fall inside the GADM outline of Kosovo, and so which ones the report already draws on. It cannot show what any museum holds and has not published, which is precisely the material the desk review had in mind.

The museums’ own holdings outside GBIF were not examined: none of their catalogues was searched and no one was asked. Specimens labelled “Serbia” or “Yugoslavia” from before 2008 are a second blind spot; they only surface in a Kosovo search if their coordinates are tested against the outline.

What it adds for Natura 2000 in Kosovo

Historical specimens are the baseline against which losses are judged, and for many invertebrates and plants they are the only evidence of occurrence. For a sufficiency ledger (question 4) an old specimen from a site is a reason to survey it, not proof that the species is still there. Most old labels give a place name only, so the data serve the national tier.

Caveats and sensitivity

  • Only GBIF was checked. Material held but not published is by definition invisible here.
  • Specimen localities of rare plants and insects are sensitive; publishing them precisely can prompt collecting.

Integration into the pipeline

Material on GBIF is already in the download when its coordinates fall inside the GADM outline. For undigitised material there is nothing to integrate until a museum publishes it. pipeline.R is not changed.
