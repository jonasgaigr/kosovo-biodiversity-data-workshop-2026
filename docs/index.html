GIS and biodiversity data management

Workshop materials from the TAIEX Expert Mission, Pristina, 28–30 September 2026

This page gathers the material from the six sessions Jonáš Gaigr of the Nature Conservation Agency of the Czech Republic gave at the mission. Every presentation is here as slides to view in the browser and as a PDF handout to download, next to the GBIF data site that several of the sessions used as their worked example.

The mission

The Expert Mission on Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and biodiversity data management was organised by TAIEX, the European Commission’s Technical Assistance and Information Exchange instrument, in co-operation with the Ministry of Environment, Spatial Planning and Infrastructure (case ID ETT IND/EXP 82606). For three days, experts from the Nature Conservation Agency of the Czech Republic and the Ministry of Environment of the Slovak Republic worked with the institutions that hold Kosovo’s* biodiversity data.

Its aim was practical: to show how GIS supports environmental management, biodiversity conservation and the implementation of the Birds and Habitats Directives. That covered spatial analysis, habitat mapping, satellite and remote sensing data, the monitoring of protected areas, assessing the conservation status of habitats and species, and identifying Natura 2000 sites. Alongside it, the mission set out to bring spatial data management in line with EU standards and to strengthen co-operation between the institutions that collect and use the data.

* This designation is without prejudice to positions on status, and is in line with UNSCR 1244/1999 and the ICJ Opinion on the Kosovo declaration of independence.
